Use of multirotor UAVs for rapid field documentation, orthomosaic production, mapping and visual inspection in water-resources operations. The approach is designed to complement traditional field inspection with repeatable geospatial evidence, especially in areas where access, scale or terrain limit ground observation.

The operational focus is on **simple, robust and auditable workflows** rather than unnecessary complexity: flight planning, georeferenced imagery, GIS integration and technical interpretation for decision support.
