**Project status:** In development — geospatial data integration and workflow design.


A geospatial workflow for **groundwater prospecting in crystalline terrain**, integrating geological mapping, hydrogeological domains, digital elevation models, structural lineaments, drainage patterns and available well data.

The project focuses on building a transparent evidence stack for reconnaissance and prioritization. Geophysics remains a complementary field method; GIS is used to narrow the search area and make site-selection reasoning explicit.
