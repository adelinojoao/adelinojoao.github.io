**Project status:** Workflow in development — document screening and validation rules.


Concept for an automated document-screening workflow supporting water-resource licensing and permit analysis. The system is designed to identify required documents, apply predefined validation rules, flag inconsistencies and generate a concise internal review output.

The project explores where automation can reduce repetitive work **without replacing technical judgment**, keeping the process traceable and suitable for public-sector workflows.
