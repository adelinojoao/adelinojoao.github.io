01 · About

Water, territory
and technology.

Technical decisions in water management are rarely made under laboratory conditions. They happen amid scarcity, competing demands, imperfect data, field constraints and institutional accountability.

My approach is to combine consolidated field practice with geospatial and digital tools where they genuinely improve the decision. GIS, remote sensing, drones, automation and instrumentation are useful only when they make monitoring more reliable, evidence more defensible, or operations simpler.
